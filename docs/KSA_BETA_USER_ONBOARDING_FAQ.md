# myCHAT / myGENE Kabayan KSA Beta — User Onboarding Instructions and FAQs

**Program:** KSA closed beta for first 100 OFWs  
**Audience:** OFWs in Saudi Arabia joining the controlled beta  
**Positioning:** Human-supported pilot. Not an emergency authority.

---

## 1. Welcome Message for Beta Users

Welcome, Kabayan.

You are invited to join the **myCHAT / myGENE Kabayan KSA closed beta** for the first 100 OFWs in Saudi Arabia.

This beta helps you:

- Ask OFW questions in one chat.
- Create a case file for urgent or work-related concerns.
- Keep a checklist of important documents.
- Find OWWA, DMW, Embassy, Consulate, MWO, and emergency guidance.
- Track benefits, remittance, family support, and follow-ups.
- Help the team improve the product before a wider launch.

Important:

> myCHAT / myGENE Kabayan is a beta support and routing tool. It is not a police, ambulance, embassy, DMW, OWWA, MWO, or emergency authority. If you are in immediate danger in Saudi Arabia, contact local emergency services first: **999 Police**, **997 Ambulance**, or **998 Fire**. Then contact the Philippine Embassy / Consulate / MWO, OWWA 1348, DMW, or 1343 for trafficking indicators.

---

## 2. Who Can Join This Beta

This first beta is for up to **100 OFWs in Saudi Arabia**.

You may join if:

- You are currently in Saudi Arabia or preparing to work there.
- You understand this is a beta test, not a final government service.
- You agree to the beta consent and privacy notice.
- You are willing to give feedback about what is helpful, confusing, missing, or wrong.
- You understand that urgent danger should go to local emergency services first.

---

## 3. Before You Start

Prepare the following, if available:

- Your current KSA city, such as Riyadh, Jeddah, Al Khobar, Dammam, or another city.
- A safe callback number or masked contact number.
- Passport copy or passport details, if needed for your own record.
- Employment contract.
- Employer and agency details.
- Iqama / visa / work permit information.
- Salary records, payslips, or screenshots if you have a salary concern.
- Chat messages, photos, audio, location, or other evidence if you have a safety concern.

Do not share sensitive documents in public group chats. Only provide sensitive information through the approved beta intake or secure case process.

## 4. Step-by-Step Onboarding

### Step 1 — Open myCHAT / myGENE Kabayan

Open the app and go to the **myGENE Kabayan** area.

You will see these beta sections:

- **Beta** — enrollment and consent gate.
- **Chat** — ask OFW questions naturally.
- **SOS** — urgent safety or emergency case creation.
- **Cases** — follow-up tracker for created cases.
- **Docs** — document checklist.
- **Benefits** — OWWA, DMW, AKSYON, NRCO, and other benefit guidance.
- **Remit** — family finance and remittance tracker.
- **Countries** — Saudi Arabia and other country guides.
- **Providers** — government, remittance, cargo, and support directory.
- **Ops** — staff/operator area; users normally do not need this.

### Step 2 — Read the Beta Consent

In the **Beta** tab, read and accept each consent item:

1. Privacy notice and what data is stored for the beta.
2. Emergency boundary: myCHAT is not an emergency authority.
3. Data processing for your beta profile, cases, and support notes.
4. Family notification choice.

You cannot enroll until all required consent items are checked.

### Step 3 — Enroll in the First-100 Roster

Enter:

- Your name.
- Your KSA city.
- Your safe callback number or masked phone.

Tap **Enroll slot**.

You will receive a beta code like:

```text
KSA-BETA-001
KSA-BETA-002
...
KSA-BETA-100
```

Keep this beta code for support follow-up.

### Step 4 — Use Chat for Normal Questions

Use the **Chat** tab for questions like:

- “How do I renew OWWA membership?”
- “My salary is delayed. What should I prepare?”
- “Where should I ask for help in Riyadh?”
- “How can I organize my OFW documents?”
- “How should I split remittance for family and savings?”

The chatbot will classify your concern and suggest next steps.

### Step 5 — Use SOS for Urgent or High-Risk Concerns

Use **SOS** if your concern involves:

- Passport confiscation.
- Being locked in or unable to leave.
- Abuse, threat, harassment, or violence.
- Detention or arrest.
- Forced work or trafficking indicators.
- Medical danger.
- No safe place to stay.

If there is immediate danger, contact Saudi emergency services first:

```text
999 Police
997 Ambulance
998 Fire
937 Health hotline
```

Then create a case file in the app and contact the appropriate Philippine support channel.

### Step 6 — Create a Case File

In **SOS**, fill in:

- OFW name.
- Country / city.
- Issue summary.
- Employer, if safe to record.
- Agency, if known.
- Whether family notification is authorized.

Tap **Create case file**.

A case file may include:

- Case ID.
- Issue summary.
- Urgency level.
- Suggested referral path.
- Evidence checklist.
- Follow-up reminder.

### Step 7 — Track Follow-Ups

Go to **Cases** to see your case file.

Use the case file to track:

- What happened.
- What evidence you have.
- Who should follow up.
- What referral path was suggested.
- Whether family notification is authorized.

### Step 8 — Use Docs as a Checklist

The **Docs** tab is a checklist for important OFW records:

- Passport.
- Visa / work permit.
- Verified employment contract.
- OEC / OFW clearance.
- OWWA membership proof.
- Payslips / wage records.
- Remittance receipts.
- Medical records / insurance.

For this beta, treat the checklist as a reminder. Do not upload sensitive documents unless the official beta process tells you where and how.

### Step 9 — Give Feedback

During the beta, staff may ask:

- Was the app easy to understand?
- Did the guidance match your situation?
- Were any phone numbers, offices, or steps missing?
- Did any wording feel confusing or unsafe?
- What would make this more useful for OFWs in KSA?

Your feedback helps improve the product before a wider launch.

## 5. Safety Rules for Users

1. **Immediate danger first:** Call Saudi emergency services before using the app.
2. **Do not confront an abusive employer because the app suggested evidence collection.** Prioritize safety.
3. **Use safe communication.** If your phone is monitored, avoid writing sensitive details.
4. **Keep documents backed up.** Store copies safely with someone you trust.
5. **Do not post passport, phone, employer address, or case details in public groups.**
6. **Use family notification only if you authorize it.**
7. **Report wrong information immediately.** The KSA directory must stay verified.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is myCHAT / myGENE Kabayan a government service?

No. It is a beta support and routing tool. It can guide you toward official channels such as Embassy, Consulate, MWO, OWWA, DMW, or 1343, but it does not replace those offices.

### Q2. Is this an emergency hotline?

No. If you are in immediate danger in Saudi Arabia, contact local emergency services first:

```text
999 Police
997 Ambulance
998 Fire
937 Health hotline
```

After that, use the app to organize your case and follow-up information.

### Q3. What does “first 100 OFWs” mean?

This beta is limited to the first 100 enrolled OFWs in KSA. The limit keeps the pilot manageable and allows staff to monitor cases safely.

### Q4. What is my beta code?

Your beta code is your pilot participant ID, such as `KSA-BETA-001`. Use it when communicating with beta staff.

### Q5. What should I do if my passport was taken?

If you are safe, create an SOS case and prepare:

- Passport copy if available.
- Contract.
- Employer and agency details.
- Chat screenshots or messages.
- Current city and safe callback number.

If you are in danger, contact Saudi emergency services first.

### Q6. What should I do if my salary is unpaid?

Create a case and prepare:

- Contract.
- Payslips.
- Attendance records.
- Employer messages.
- Remittance records.
- Number of unpaid months and promised salary.

The app can route this as a labor concern for MWO / DMW style follow-up.

### Q7. Can the app contact my family?

Only if you authorize family notification. During onboarding and case creation, you can choose whether family notification may be prepared.

### Q8. Will my information be private?

The beta is designed to minimize sensitive data and use redacted exports for staff review. However, this is still a beta. Do not share unnecessary passport numbers, full phone numbers, employer private addresses, or sensitive documents in public or unsecured channels.

### Q9. Can I use this for remittance?

The **Remit** section helps you plan family support, savings, personal expenses, and OFW funds. It does not replace a licensed remittance provider.

### Q10. Can I use this outside Saudi Arabia?

The product may include other country guides, but this beta is specifically for KSA OFWs. If you are outside KSA, staff may ask you to wait for a future pilot.

### Q11. What if the app gives wrong information?

Tell beta staff immediately. Do not rely on unverified contact numbers or outdated office details. Staff must verify official KSA contact information before launch and keep it updated.

### Q12. What if I want to leave the beta?

Contact beta staff and ask to withdraw. Staff should mark your beta profile as withdrawn and stop non-essential follow-up.

### Q13. What languages can I use?

You may write in English, Filipino/Tagalog, or simple mixed language. Use clear details: city, issue, date, employer/agency if safe, and what help you need.

### Q14. What should I never type into the app?

Avoid entering sensitive details unless necessary and unless the official beta process confirms it is safe:

- Full passport number.
- Full home address.
- Full employer private address.
- Bank passwords or OTPs.
- Private family details not needed for the case.
- Anything that could put you at risk if someone else sees your phone.

### Q15. Who reviews urgent cases?

Human beta operators review cases during assigned coverage. The app helps route and organize, but human staff are responsible for escalation and follow-up.
